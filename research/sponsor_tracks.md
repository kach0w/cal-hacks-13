# Cal Hacks 13.0 sponsor tracks (for Recall)

> Researched at ~5:00 AM PDT, before the integrations were built. Notes here about what Recall "currently" has
> (for example "zero Claude usage") describe that moment. What was built afterwards is in [final_report.md](final_report.md).

Compiled 2026-10-25 ~5:00 AM PDT from `/tmp/recall-research/out/research.json` (46 researched tracks plus general findings)
and the official track list on Devpost, https://cal-hacks-13-0.devpost.com/ (saved as `/tmp/recall-research/hack-home.txt`).
The live site is Hive, https://hive.hackberkeley.org/prizes. Its data comes from https://hive.hackberkeley.org/api/live/prizes.
Ignore live.calhacks.io: it still serves the Cal Hacks 12.0 site.

How to read this table:
- "VERIFIED" means the text was seen on the cited official page. "Not found" means the researchers looked and found nothing. "UNVERIFIED" means it was inferred.
- Devpost "$ in cash" figures for non-cash prizes look like Devpost's valuation of the prize, not real cash.
- Fit is fit to Recall as it is now: a local, camera-based memory of where things are for people with dementia, plus a care-partner page, a BLE band, Moss and Tiger Data.
- Effort is extra hours from now.
- Tracks are listed in Devpost order. The only track that is not on Devpost, [MLH] Best Use of Gen AI, is at the end and marked UNVERIFIED ON DEVPOST.

Duplicates merged: the researchers and scorers wrote some tracks under slightly different names. These are one track each:
- Visa: "Best use of Reimagining Shopping" = "Reimagining Shopping"
- Elastic: "Best use of the Elastic Agent Builder on a Serverless Instance" = "Agent Builder on Serverless"
- Apify: "Best Actor sponsored by Apify" = "Best Actor"
- SpaceXSI: "Make it Legendary with SpaceXSI" = "Make it Legendary"
- Hyphenate: "The Hyphenated Enterprise: Autonomous Systems for Finance" = "Autonomous Systems for Finance"
- MLH Gen AI: three spellings of "Best Use of Gen AI (Hive listing)"

Hive calls SpaceXSI "SpaceXAI". Figma's Hive name is "Figma in the Workflow".
